What is this page?

This page lists restaurants in St. Augustine, Florida recommended by Nesta Systems, the St. Augustine small business consulting company that built and maintains watchstaugustine.com. Every restaurant listed is a place the Nesta team sends its own friends and clients; listings are free and no restaurant pays to appear. The list currently includes The Ice Plant, 17 King, The Floridian and La Nouvelle. Each listing gives the address, hours from the restaurant's own website, what it is known for, a link to the restaurant's site, and the story of why the Nesta Systems team recommends it. The site's free live cameras of downtown and the beaches let visitors check weather and crowds before going out.

How we choose

We love going out when we can. What we're really chasing is great food, exceptional service, and a place that sets the vibe for the whole experience, whether that's brunch, a late lunch, or a big night out. St. Augustine has grown into an incredible food town, and these pages are our way of celebrating the places we find the most outstanding and telling everyone we can about them.

The rule is simple: a place makes the list only if we'd send a friend or family member there. Every pick is somewhere we already go. We write our own short note on why, then we walk in and tell the owner they made our list. Listings are free, always. Hours and details come from each restaurant's own website, so confirm before a special trip. If a place is missing, it's either on our list to visit or it isn't somewhere we'd recommend yet.
