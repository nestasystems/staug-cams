HOW TO USE
Each business below gets two free things: a badge for their website, and a live-camera link.
The badge image must be uploaded to your site at https://watchstaugustine.com/badge.png (file: badge.png, in this folder).
Copy the block for the business, email or text it to them, and that's it.
